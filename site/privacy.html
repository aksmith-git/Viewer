Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

The short version

Viewer does not collect, store or share any information about you or your files.

Your files

Files you open are read on your device only. Viewer never uploads them anywhere. Its list of recent files is kept on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

No tracking

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No third-party services or SDKs that collect data.

Web content

Viewer itself makes no network requests. A web page you open may load resources from the internet (for example fonts or scripts), exactly as it would in a browser; those requests go directly from your device to those sites under their own privacy policies. Links to websites open in Safari.

Children

Viewer collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
